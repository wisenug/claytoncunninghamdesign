A collection of moving things, compiled from various projects.

Ask Autotrader

Ask Autotrader is Cox Automotive's AI shopping assistant, built into the car search experience. These are a few of the motion pieces I designed for it: a thinking indicator, in-context product moments, and small character traits that give the assistant some personality, and help to anthropomorphize.

Small character traits give the assistant some personality. The mark pulses and bounces to keep the assistant feeling alive.

Kelley Blue Book

Kelley Blue Book's take on the AI chatbot. I animated the logo according to the brand's attributes, using a personified thinking animation.

Motion should bring added value to the overall product experience, while connecting users into accompanying narratives, while telling a unified story.
